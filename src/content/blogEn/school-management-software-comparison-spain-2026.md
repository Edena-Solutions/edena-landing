<strong>School management software in Spain compared: 8 platforms, criterion by criterion</strong>

<br>

If you are choosing software for your school in Spain, you will probably end up comparing some of these platforms: Edena, Alexia, Clickedu, Esemtia, SM Educamos, TokApp, Dinantia and LiveKid. They do not all do the same thing. Some started as communication tools for families, others as academic management systems and others for nurseries.

When we say something "does not appear in its public information", it means we did not find it in those sources, not that the platform lacks it.

<br>

<strong>Three types of platform</strong>

<br>

Before comparing features, it helps to know what kind of tool each one is:

- **Family communication apps:** TokApp and Dinantia. They handle notices, messages, authorisations and payments from families. Their public information does not show a complete student record or VERI*FACTU invoicing.
- **Academic and school management platforms:** Alexia, Clickedu, Esemtia and SM Educamos. They cover student records, assessment, a virtual classroom and financial management, and are usually linked to publishing or education groups.
- **Nursery apps:** LiveKid, focused on the day-to-day of children aged 0 to 3.

<strong>How to choose for your type of school</strong>

<br>

- **Nursery:** what matters is the daily diary for families (meals, naps, photos), the waiting list and fee collection with grants or subsidised places. Also compare how invoicing works and whether the system is ready for Verifactu.
- **Private or state-funded school:** you need assessment and report cards, several services per student (lunch, bus, extracurricular activities), invoicing with remittances and reliable communication with families. If your region has its own education platform, ask how it connects.
- **Academy:** enrolment is continuous, fees vary by activity and number of classes, and recruitment carries more weight. Look at the CRM, class packs and sibling discounts.
- **Education group:** look for reports across the network and the ability for each school to have its own invoice series.

<br>

<strong>What to ask for in the demo</strong>

<br>

A demo with sample data shows the screens, not whether the platform fits your school. Ask the vendor to walk you through:

1. **An enrolment application** from your website form to the student's registration.
2. **A real bill** from your school, with several services, a discount and its Verifactu record.
3. **An unpaid bill:** how the family is reminded and how it shows on the dashboard.
4. **A notice to the families** of a single year group, with read receipts.
5. **The migration:** which data is imported, in which format and who does it.

Ask for the answers in writing and compare the total cost over three years (licence, setup, training and the tools you would have to keep alongside), not just the price per student.
